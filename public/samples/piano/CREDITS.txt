Changes made for this app: we kept every third semitone (A0 to C8) in two
loudness layers (original layers v5 and v12), removed the silence at the
start of each note, shortened each note to between 2.5 and 7 seconds with a
fade out, and converted the files to 80 kbps MP3. The app pitch-shifts these
recordings to play the notes in between.
