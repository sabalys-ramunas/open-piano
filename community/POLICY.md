# Content policy

This catalog shares piano lessons: the notes of an arrangement, with a title and a few details. This page explains what may be shared and how anyone who owns the rights to music can ask for a lesson to be removed.

**This policy is not legal advice.** Copyright rules differ from country to country. If you are unsure whether you may share something, ask someone qualified, or do not share it.

## Notes only

- A lesson file holds notes, timing and simple details (title, artist, level, tags, licence, credit). Nothing else.
- **No audio.** Lesson files never contain a recording or any part of one. The catalog may name the recording a lesson was timed against (its file name and size) so players can add their own copy, but it never links to, hosts or describes where to get that recording.
- **No lyrics.** Not in the lesson, not in the title, not in the credit.
- **No images, links to downloads, scripts or anything else.** The catalog check refuses files with any field it does not know.

## What may be shared

You may submit a lesson only if you have the right to share it under the licence you choose. In practice that means one of these:

- **Public-domain music.** For example most classical music, traditional folk songs and hymns, where the composer died long enough ago. Note that a modern edition, arrangement or recording of old music can have its own rights.
- **Your own music**, or music whose rights holder has given you permission to share a lesson of it.
- **Openly licensed music** whose licence allows sharing and changes (for example CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA). Follow its terms: give credit, and keep share-alike licences.

When you submit a lesson, you confirm that you made it (or have permission from the person who did), that you have the right to share it, and that it contains notes only.

Maintainers decline any lesson whose rights are unclear.

## Removal requests (takedown)

If you hold rights in music that a lesson in this catalog uses, and you have not allowed it, we will remove the lesson.

How to ask:

1. Open an issue in the catalog repository titled "Takedown request".
2. Tell us which lesson (its title or id), what work it is based on, and that you are the rights holder or are acting for them.

What happens next:

- Maintainers aim to reply within 7 days.
- While a request is looked at, the lesson is hidden from the index.
- If the request is valid, the lesson file and its index entry are deleted. If the person who submitted it believes the removal was a mistake, they can reply with their reasons, and maintainers will decide in good faith.
- Lessons that people have already downloaded stay on their own devices; the catalog cannot reach into anyone's app.

## Everything else

Maintainers may remove any lesson that breaks this policy, contains offensive titles or credits, or is spam. Repeated deliberate breaches lead to future submissions being declined.
